Why this journey

Kenya's northern frontier, experienced as a journey.

This is not simply a transfer from one destination to another. The route moves through changing landscapes, remote communities and remarkable natural settings — from the forests and crater-lake landscape of Marsabit to Chalbi's salt flats, North Horr's dunes and Lake Turkana.

The journey includes photography, desert walks, cultural experiences, a Lake Turkana boat experience and time beside the lake, while professional guides, drivers and photography support the group throughout.

The route

Day by day

Day 01
10 Dec

Nairobi → Nanyuki → Samburu → Marsabit

Depart Nairobi at 3:30 AM. Stop at the Equator and Cedar Mall in Nanyuki before the northern drive. Continue via Samburu and Laisamis, with a photography stop overlooking Mount Ololokwe.

Explore Marsabit National Park, including its crater-lake landscape, subject to road and park conditions. Dinner and overnight in Marsabit.

Day 02
11 Dec

Marsabit → Maikona → Chalbi Desert → North Horr

After breakfast, leave Marsabit for the Chalbi region. Experience the white salt flats and vast desert landscape through photography, content creation, desert walks and landscape viewing.

Continue to North Horr for lunch and check-in. Later, head to the sand dunes for an afternoon/evening experience and sunset, followed by dinner and a bonfire under the northern sky.

Day 03
12 Dec

North Horr → Loiyangalani → Lake Turkana

An optional sunrise dune experience is available at 6:00 AM. After breakfast, depart for Loiyangalani and Lake Turkana.

Take a Lake Turkana boat experience, with boats operating in groups of approximately eight, and learn more about the local El Molo community and its cultural heritage, subject to local arrangements and conditions. Afternoon lakeside relaxation includes swimming, photography and sunset views.

Day 04
13 Dec

Loiyangalani → Wind Farm → Ngurunit → Laisamis → Isiolo

Depart early and travel towards Ngurunit, passing around the Lake Turkana Wind Power project, with a photography stop subject to conditions and timing.

Continue to Ngurunit and the natural sliding rocks, subject to water, weather and ground conditions. Proceed through Laisamis and Archers Post to Isiolo for dinner and overnight at Sala Hotel.

Day 05
14 Dec

Isiolo → Nairobi

Breakfast and check-out, then depart Isiolo at approximately 9:30 AM. Travel via the main highway with a possible short refreshment/rest stop en route.

Arrive back in Nairobi at approximately 4:00–5:00 PM, bringing the Northern Kenya Frontier Road Trip to a close.

Travel notes

Come prepared for the frontier.

Road conditions

Expect long stretches of rough, dusty and corrugated roads. Travel times may change depending on road conditions.

Connectivity

Network coverage may be limited or unavailable in some sections. Download important documents and maps before departure.

Timing

The 3:30 AM Nairobi departure and scheduled morning departures are important because delays can affect later activities.

December conditions

The northern frontier can be hot during the day, while early mornings and evenings may feel considerably cooler.

Accommodation

Accommodation may be split according to capacity. On the Loiyangalani night, the group may stay across two properties while meals remain together.

What to bring

Bring a refillable bottle, sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, dust scarf/shuka, comfortable shoes, swimwear, towel, power bank, personal medication and identification.

Pack for the experience

Light, practical and ready for dust, sun and water.

  • Refillable water bottle & drinking water
  • Snacks
  • Sunscreen
  • Hat/cap & sunglasses
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Shuka/scarf for dust
  • Light comfortable clothing
  • Warm jacket
  • Closed & comfortable/open shoes
  • Swimwear & towel
  • Power bank & charging cables
  • Personal medication & toiletries
  • Wet wipes, sanitiser & tissues
  • Identification documents
  • Small cash in Kenyan shillings
