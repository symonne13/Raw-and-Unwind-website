The Akiba Philosophy

Travel is not a reward. It is a right.

Akiba Safaris exists because the best places in Kenya — the ones Kenyans have been told to admire from a distance — belong to us first.

Every property was selected to be within financial reach of a household, a Chama group, or an individual saver. We carry your financial wellbeing like we carry the journey: carefully, with both hands.

How It Works

Build your journey one payment at a time.

Pay a 20–30% deposit to confirm your booking.

Save monthly in instalments of up to 12 months. Your rate is frozen at booking.

On the 5th of every month, your personalised WhatsApp reminder arrives.

No penalties for life circumstances. Just a journey, built one payment at a time.
